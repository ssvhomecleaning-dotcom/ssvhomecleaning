Our Services

Home Cleaning Services in Hyderabad

Every service is scoped and quoted by phone before we begin — choose one or combine several.

Home Deep Cleaning

A thorough top-to-bottom clean of every room — floors, walls, fans, switchboards, windows and furniture surfaces. Ideal for seasonal cleaning or before hosting guests.

Kitchen Cleaning

De-greasing of chimneys, hobs, tiles, cabinets and countertops, so cooking spaces stay hygienic and odor-free.

Bathroom Cleaning

Descaling of taps and showerheads, tile and grout cleaning, and sanitization of fittings and commodes.

Sofa & Upholstery Cleaning

Shampooing and stain treatment for sofas, mattresses, and carpets using fabric-safe methods.

Water Tank Cleaning

Sludge removal and sanitization of overhead and underground water tanks to keep water supply clean.

Apartment & Villa Cleaning

Full-property cleaning for move-in, move-out, or scheduled periodic upkeep of apartments and villas.
